# 2026-11-04: `W3FLX2` at bit parity

`W3FLX2`, the Tolman and Chalikov (1996) stress with a drag that depends on the peak frequency (`FLX2`, with `ST2`), is
translated into `ww3-kernels` (`flx2.rs`) with SPIR-V and PTX entry points and no new `ww3-libm` function. Status `routine`
(`rust/port_report/W3FLX2.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings.

## Result

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| `just capture flx2 <variant> <depth>` for 11 captures of `switch_chaceon_st2` | 27 295 calls, 2500, 50, 12, 10, 8 and 6 m, winds 0 to 60 m/s, and pokes |
| `just flx2-cmp` on the 11 | 0 of 109 180 values differ, call and batch layouts; `DSIE`, `N1MAX` and `EWN1` rebuilt by `DISTAB` equal |
| `just spirv-replay flx2` on the 11 | 0 differ, lavapipe |
| `just spirv`, `just ptx`, `just check`, `just lint-sh` | pass; PTX has 9 `div.rn.f32` and no float `fma` or `mad` |
| 37 fault runs (`rust/port_report/W3FLX2.md`) | all caught but two: a `MAX(+0,-0)` that no site sees, and a control that is equivalent |

## What the captures and the pokes reached

`ww3_ts1` at 2500 m is all deep water for `W3FLX2`: `SIX/DSIE` is above `N1MAX = 120`, and the table is never read. A 10 m run
reads the table on 6851 of 7255 calls, and 6 m reaches 1455 of 1500. A 60 m/s wind floors `RDCH` on every call, a calm run floors `U` on
every call, and the poke reaches the `FP`, `UST` and `DEPTH` floors that the model never does (`FP` is not under 0.001 in a run). The depth
of `ww3_ts1` is bounded below by `DMIN = 5.75`, so a 3 or 4 m run leaves the grid dry and records nothing.

## Two traps

- The ninth argument, `CD`, is on the stack on arm64, not in `x8`: the first capture script read it from `x8`, wrote nine values and
  stopped silently. `regtests/capture/sbt4.py` has the same layout for its arguments past eight.
- A fault literal must differ in `f32`: `0.0010000001` rounds to the same `f32` as `0.001`, and the first fault of that constant
  changed nothing. `0.0010000002` is one ULP up.

## Not done

`W3FLX2` is not called from `W3SRCE`. The capped captures (1500 calls) sample only the start of a run ⚠. `NITTIN` and `CINXSI` were
captured only at 3 and 0.20 ⚠.
