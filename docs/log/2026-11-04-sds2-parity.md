## Reading the compiled code first

The routine compiled alone in `gcc:12-bookworm` (gfortran 12.2) with `-DW3_ST2 -O3 -ffp-contract=off
-fdump-tree-optimized`, `SIG`, `DDEN` and the `W3GDATMD` scalars stubbed as module variables, gave the order of every
operation, and `objdump` showed `fmaxnm` and `fminnm` at all `MAX` and `MIN` sites, `fcvtzs` for `INT` and calls to `logf`,
`powf` (three sites) and `fmodf`. The first replay of the first capture was already 0 differ.

## What the captures reach

At 12 m/s `ww3_ts1` reaches the low-frequency `HW`, `IKHW = NK` with and without the `MAX(0., XHW-0.5)` clamp, and the
high-frequency `HW` on a handful of calls; a 16-band grid reaches the high-frequency branch on every call. `SDS2`
namelist variants run the three `PHIMIN` branches of `w3gridmd.F90:2143-2160`. `POW` at its cap of 25 and the exact tie
`FHW = SIG(NK+1)` are reached by the entry poke only (`regtests/capture/sds2.py`), where the faults for them are caught.
The calm run has an empty spectrum and zero `S` and `D`.
