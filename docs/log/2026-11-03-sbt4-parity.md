## Reading the compiled code first

gfortran inlines `TABU_ERF` into `W3SBT4` and peels its first pass: `erf(-4.)` is folded to `-1.` at compile time,
and the other 300 entries call `erff`. glibc's `erff(-4.)` is `-1.` as well, so a fault there catches nothing. The
glibc functions fuse where GCC could: each Horner step of `erff` is an `fmadd`, read with `objdump` from the image's
`libm.so.6`. A wrong constant (`16*efx` written with exponent +6, not +4) showed at once as 134 217 726 subnormal
inputs that differed; the sweep found it, not the captures, whose arguments never go below 2^-28.

## Weak faults, each explained

`ww3_ts1` at 8 m keeps `PSIN2` clamped to `SBTCX(3)` on every call, so a fault in its term changes nothing there; the
storm capture reaches it, and even one ULP of `SQRT(TPI)` then moves a `BEDFORM` value. `ECOS(IS)` and `ECOS(ITH)`
agree because `W3GRID` repeats the cosines across bands. In the ripple branch the sheet-flow roughness is too small
next to the ripple roughness for a regrouping to survive their sum: that one association rests on the disassembly
alone ⚠.

## `spirv_replay srce` had been broken since 2026-10-31

Running every SPIR-V replay after the shader changed, `srce` differed by up to 7·10^7 ULP, at `HEAD` without this
change as well. Commit `a1ac089` gave the `srce` entry point a 54th buffer, `active`, and skips a point whose flag is
0; the replay bound 53, so most points never ran. The whole-run pairs bind it, which is why `scripts/spirv_pair.sh`
kept passing. `spirv_replay` now binds `active`, all ones, and `srce` is 0 differ on default 8 m and storm 6 m. A
replay suite that is not rerun after a shader change can rot silently: every replay ran here.

## Not done

reference's `ww3_grid` stops with status 3 there.
