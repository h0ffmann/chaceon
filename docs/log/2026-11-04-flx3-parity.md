# 2026-11-04: `W3FLX3` at bit parity, and what a capture of the floors needs

`W3FLX3`, the Tolman and Chalikov (1996) stress with a cap on the drag coefficient (`FLX3`, used with `ST2`), is a three-pass
iteration over `logf`, two `powf` and `sqrtf`, then a minimum or a hyperbolic tangent. Status `routine`
(`rust/port_report/W3FLX3.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings.

## Result

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| `just capture flx3 <variant> <depth> <stride>` for 14 captures | 6897 calls from the `switch_chaceon_st2flx3` reference (sampled: stride 4 to 40 on the model runs, 1 on the poke) |
| `just flx3-cmp` on the 14 | 0 of 27 588 values differ; call and batch layouts agree |
| 34 fault runs | 29 caught; 5 undetected: 2 weak (redone stronger and caught) and 3 unreachable (below) |

## What the captures needed

`ww3_ts1` at 2500 m reaches only the deep branch of the inlined `WAVNU1` (`SIX/DSIE` is about 490 against `N1MAX = 120`), so the table
branch needs a shallow run: at 8 m it is 240 of 248 calls. The cap of `2.5E-3` acts at 30 m/s and almost never at 12 m/s, so the
default capture reaches the minimum once, and `CDMAX` of `1.E-3` (`flx3cap`) makes it act on every call. `CTYPE = 1` gives the tangent
(`flx3tanh`, `flx3tanhdef`), `CDMAX = 0` gives `CD = 0` (`flx3zero`). The floors of `FP`, `U` and `UST` are out of reach of a run
(`ww3_ts1` raises a zero wind to 0.001, the first call has `UST = 1.E-5`), so `flx3poke` writes other inputs at the routine's entry,
as `sin3.py` does, and puts them back at exit with the results replaced by the previous call's so the run stays finite.

The routine's ninth argument, `CD`, is on the stack at entry on aarch64: the first capture script read `x8` and recorded 3 of the 4 results.

calls of a run, not all of them ⚠.
