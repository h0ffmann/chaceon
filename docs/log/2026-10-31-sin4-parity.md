# W3SIN4, W3SPR4 and ST4's tables: a folded constant and unreachable guards

2026-10-31, agent session `cd86e8f5` for the owner; no issue. Branch
`goal/cd86e8f5-6138-sin4-parity`. Results: `rust/port_report/W3SIN4.md`.

## Question

Can ST4's input and its setup be ported at the reference's bits, so no table comes from Fortran? And
are MetOcean's changes, the reason this reference was chosen, actually shown?

## Result

- `W3SIN4`, `W3SPR4` and `CALC_USTAR` match on every captured call (v). Every ST4 table, built in Rust
  from namelist values, equals the reference's (v). `log10f` joins `ww3-libm`, at parity on all inputs.
- MetOcean's `USTAR` floor and `ISNAN` guards in `W3SIN4` are shown by entry pokes only (v).

## What went wrong on the way

- **All-zero tables passed.** `DCKI` and `QBI` matched on default settings because they are empty there.
  The first populated capture differed in 1 998 values.
- **A compiler folded one row.** All 1 998 were `IKD=1`. `-O3` peels the first pass of `DO IKD=1,NKD`
  and folds `1.01**(-999)` to `0x384a1eff`; `__powisf2` gives `0x384a1eb3`. Shown by exact arithmetic, by
  a small gfortran loop that prints the two values, and by `INSIN4`'s two `tanhf` calls around one
  `__powisf2`.
- **"Low wind reaches the floors" was wrong.** `calm` and `light` were added for that, and the same
  faults still passed, because `W3SRCE` floors `USTAR` at 0.001. A summary line of mine printed
  `differ,` from the wrong field, and it was the `PARITY` at the end that showed the truth.
- **The `0.3` reasoning was wrong too.** `(TAUX**2+TAUY**2)**0.25` is about `UST`, so `MIN` rarely picks
  the limit. It is still unreached, and listed as such.
- **ULP misled on SPIR-V.** Millions of ULP look alarming, and they sit on values that nearly cancel:
  the replay now also reports the difference against the array's peak, about 1e-6.

`ww3_ts1`. `W3SRCE` calls `W3SDB1`, `W3SBT1`, `W3SLN1` and the mean-parameter code, so those come with it.
