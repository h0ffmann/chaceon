# W3SDS4 at host parity, ww3-libm, and a lavapipe limit

2026-10-31, agent session `cd86e8f5` for the owner; no issue. Branch
`goal/cd86e8f5-2964-sds4-parity`. Results: `rust/port_report/W3SDS4.md`.

## Question

Does the capture route of ADR-0007 reach a routine with three schemes and most of libm, and does a
kernel of that size survive both GPU compilers with its parity?

## Result

- `W3SDS4` matches the reference on all 12 452 captured calls across 10 captures, 32 325 392 values, in
  call and batch layouts (v).
- `ww3-libm` equals glibc 2.36 on aarch64 on every `f32` for `expf`, `logf`, `sqrtf`, `expm1f`, `tanhf`,
  `atanf`, `sin`, `cos`, and on 8 * 10^9 pairs each for `powf` and `atan2f` (v, `just libm-bits`).
- SPIR-V and PTX build (v). The SPIR-V is **not** bit-identical on lavapipe (table in the report), and
  `W3SNL1`'s still is (v).

## What went wrong on the way

- **glibc fuses.** Plain Rust gave 1 ULP differences in `expm1f`, `atanf`, `atan2f`, `tanhf`, then on 12
  and 22 of 2^32 inputs in `sin`, `cos`, and 1 of 10^9 pairs in `powf`. `objdump` of the reference's
  `libm.so.6` gave the sites. `expf` and `logf` match without any.
- **A comment was wrong.** fdlibm's `aT[0]` comment says `0x3eaaaaaa`; the decimal gives `0x3eaaaaab`,
  as the machine code loads. Taken from the comment, it hid behind the fused-site differences until
  the disassembly was read.
- **A lavapipe limit looked like a port bug.** `SRHS` was off by 35 million ULP on SPIR-V. Three
  hypotheses failed: libm ULPs, the by-value copy of `SSDSC`, the struct layout (read with `spirv-dis`).
  Writing iteration counters from the shader showed every loop stop after one pass from one point on.
  `LP_MAX_TGSI_LOOP_ITERATIONS` is 65535 per invocation in Mesa 22.3.6
  (`src/gallium/auxiliary/gallivm/lp_bld_limits.h:84`). `docker/Dockerfile.lavapipe` raises it.
- **"At most 5 ULP" was premature.** That was one capture. Across all ten, single values reach 12 288
  ULP.
- A batch replay on the host was added to rule out offsets, and stays.

## What it changes

`W3SIN4` and `INSIN4` come next, with the tables `W3SDS4` still reads from captures. `spirv` needs
tolerances from a real device, not from lavapipe ⚠.
