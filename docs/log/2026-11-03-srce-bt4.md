## The capture names its build

`srce_cmp` first took `BT4` from an `sbt4_<tag>.bin` beside the `W3SRCE` capture. `sbt4_default_8m.bin` came from
the `BT4` build and `srce_default_8m.bin` from the `BT1` one, so that guess was wrong for the old tags. The capture's
header now names the switch (format "SRCB"; "SRCE" files are `BT1`), and `capture.sh` uses
`switch_chaceon_bt4` for every `bt4*` variant, so the captures of one tag come from one build.

## A stash during a capture

A `git stash` to check a warning at `main` ran while captures read `scripts/` from the tree: one variant was
"unknown" and others may have used the `BT1` build. Those files were deleted and captured again. Background jobs
that read the working tree and a stash do not mix.
