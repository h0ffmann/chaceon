# 2026-11-04: `W3SPR2` at bit parity

`W3SPR2`, the mean parameters of Tolman and Chalikov (`ST2`), translated into `ww3-kernels` with SPIR-V and PTX entry
points. Status `routine` (`rust/port_report/W3SPR2.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings. It is not called from `W3SRCE`.

## Result

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| `just capture spr2 st2spr2* ...` on `chaceon-ref-capture:switch_chaceon_st2` | 11 captures, 18 829 calls: winds 0, 6, 12, 20, 30 m/s, depths 10 to 2500 m, two poke captures |
| `just spr2-cmp` on the 11 | 0 of 771 989 values differ, call and batch layouts |

The first host replay matched: the routine is sums, divisions, one `sqrtf` and a polynomial in `FPISTR` with no libm call.

## What the captures needed

`FPI` is the input of `FP`, and `FP` is what `W3FLX2` and `W3FLX3` consume, so the captures vary wind and depth: five
winds and depths from 10 to 2500 m. Those captures reach the `USTAR` and `FPISTR` floors only at the first call of the
run, and never the two `1.E-7` floors, except in calm (0.001 m/s), where `FPISTR` is under its floor on every call and
both `1.E-7` floors act on 720 of 1441 calls. `regtests/capture/spr2.py` with `CAPTURE_POKE` rewrites `USTAR`, `FPI` or `A`
at the entry of the real routine and restores them at the exit: 167 calls under the `USTAR` floor, 250 under the `FPISTR`
floor, 166 on each `1.E-7` floor in each poke capture.

## A negated spectrum and `AMAX`

`MAX(AMAX, A)` is written `if amax < av`, as in `W3SPR1`, so a `-0.` bin of the negated spectrum keeps `AMAX` at +0 on SPIR-V
too. The edit `<=` is caught by the poke captures (166 `AMAX` differ), which shows they see it.

## Not done

`W3SRCE` does not call `W3SPR2` (it is wired for `ST4`); `W3EXPO` and `GXEXPO` are not ported; a NaN spectrum and other grids
than NK 36, NTH 24 are not captured ⚠; the optimised GIMPLE was not read, since no bit differed (the disassembly of the
reference's routine has no fused instruction).
