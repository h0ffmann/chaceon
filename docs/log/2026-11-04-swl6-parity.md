## What the namelist does

`SWL6S6 = SWLB1 > 0.` and the default is `0.41E-02`, so every ST6 run calls the routine; `ww3_ts1` with `ST6` already does. The
variants `st6swl6*` set `&SWL6 CSTB1` and `SWLB1`, wind, grid sizes and the poke.

## What the captures cannot reach

`KMAX < 1.E-34` cannot be true where `ABAND > 1.E-30`, and a NaN `ABAND` makes `B1` NaN whatever `MAXLOC` returns, so faults in
those two places are not caught. Both are shown by the argument in the report, not by a capture.

## Troubles

The `calm` wind gives an all-zero spectrum: that capture proves nothing about the arithmetic. The first-entry ULP faults of
`DDEN` and `CG` were not caught (the sum swallows them); the tool now moves every entry.

## Not done

`W3SWL6` is not called from `W3SRCE`.
