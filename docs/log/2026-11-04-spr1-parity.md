# 2026-11-04: `W3SPR1` at bit parity

`W3SPR1`, the mean parameters of WAM cycle 3 (`ST1`), translated into `ww3-kernels` with SPIR-V and PTX entry points.
Status `routine` (`rust/port_report/W3SPR1.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings. It is not called from `W3SRCE`.

## Result

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| `just capture spr1 ...` on `chaceon-ref-capture:switch_chaceon_st1` | 7 captures, 8793 calls: default 2500 m and 20 m, calm, light, storm 2500 m, storm 20 m, poke |

The first host replay matched: the routine is a few sums and two divisions, with no power, no `exp` and no fold to read.

## The floors need a poke

On the six `ww3_ts1` captures the floors act on no call with a nonzero `EMEAN` (`spr1_cmp` prints the count): the spectrum is
either zero, in calm, or well above 1e-7. A fault to either floor could not have been caught there, and 0 differ would
have passed an unchecked guard. `regtests/capture/spr1.py` with `CAPTURE_POKE` rewrites `A` at the entry of the real
`W3SPR1` (scaled by 1e-12 or 1e-4, negated, or a single bin) and restores it at the exit, as `sin4.py` does for its guards.
With it 374 of 500 calls reach both floors, and the 1e-6 faults to them are caught.

## A signed zero that only SPIR-V saw

The negated spectrum has `-0.` bins. `MAX(AMAX, A)` with `AMAX = +0` must stay `+0` (gfortran's `fmaxnm`), and Rust's
`f32::max` agrees on the host, but SPIR-V's `FMax` on lavapipe returned `-0`: 125 values differed in the poke replay,
`-0` against `0`, numerically equal and different in bits. The kernel now writes the comparison, `if amax < av`, which
is `fmaxnm` for every input that can reach it. The host and SPIR-V replays are then both 0 differ. A real spectrum has no
negative bin, so no run is affected; it is recorded because "bits first" counts it.

## Capture speed

A whole-run capture at stride 1 ran at about 5 calls a second while other agents' captures ran (4210 calls in 13
minutes, then stopped). Stopped after 2399 calls, a stride-2 capture took about 2 minutes. `ww3_ts1` has 4790 `W3SPR1` calls at
2500 m. `scripts/capture.sh` now takes `CAPTURE_LIMIT=n` to stop after `n` kept calls.

## Not done

`W3SRCE` does not call `W3SPR1` (it is wired for `ST4`); `W3EXPO` and `GXEXPO` are not ported; a NaN spectrum is not captured ⚠;
the optimised GIMPLE was not read, since no bit differed.
