# 2026-11-04: `W3SIN2`, `INPTAB` and `W3BETA` at bit parity: ST2's wind input and its table

`W3SIN2`, the Tolman and Chalikov (1996) wind input of the `ST2` switch, is translated into `ww3-kernels` with SPIR-V and PTX
entry points. `INPTAB` and `W3BETA`, the interpolation table it reads, are translated on the host (`ww3-host/src/inptab.rs`)
and compared bit for bit with the reference's own table. Status `routine` for the three (`rust/port_report/W3SIN2.md`).
Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings. `W3SIN2` is not called from `W3SRCE`.

## Reading the compiled code first

The module built alone in `gcc:12-bookworm` (gfortran 12.2) with the switch's `-DW3_*` flags and
`-O3 -ffp-contract=off -fdump-tree-optimized` gives the GIMPLE of `inptab` and `w3sin2` without building the model.
`W3BETA` is inlined in `INPTAB`; the coefficients that a branch does not use are computed only inside it; the sum `A0+A4+A5` is
`A5+(A4+A0)`; the result is multiplied by `1.E-4`. In `W3SIN2`, `(1.-TRANSF)` is a divisor of 0.25 and compiles to `*4.`;
`SIN1A` is a heap array whose first loop can be fused with the second. Both routines were 0 differ on their first replay.

## What the captures reach, and what they cannot

`ww3_ts1` runs at 270 degrees (45 in one variant) and 0 to 30 m/s, and 12 m/s keeps `OMA` far inside the table
(`+-40`): `IOMA` reaches `+-NRSIGA` only with a poke of `U` to 300. `ICL` reaches 1 and 20 with the weak and the strong winds. `FPI`
never falls below about 1 rad/s in a run (v: the minimum over the captures without calm and pokes is 1.03), so `NKFIL2` is never negative; the poke writes `A = 1` in the first frequency only
and 0 above, with `U = 100`, so that `FPI = SIG(1)`. A poke of `CD` to a negative value gives `USTAR = NaN`, and a NaN in `A(1)`
gives `S(1) = NaN`, which `MAX(0.,S)` has to skip (arm64's `fmaxnm`): the plain-compare fault of `fmaxnm` is caught only there.

Two grids (`NK = 40` with `XFR = 1.07`, and `XFR = 1.05`) separate `FACTI1` from `FACTI2`. `SWELLF=1` makes the filter a no-op
(`FSWELL*D > D` in 0 calls), `SWELLF=0` and the default act in every call.

## Not done

- `FSWELL > 1` (`W3GRID` resets it), `NTH` other than 24, `FPIMIN` other than 0.009 and a `ZWND` other than 10 and 20 are not
  captured ⚠.
- `INT(OMA/DSIGA)` out of the range of `i32` is undefined in Fortran and in SPIR-V; no capture reaches it ⚠.
- `W3SIN2` is compared with the reference's table, not with the host's `inptab()`; the two tables are equal, so the
  consequence is only that no replay builds the table itself ⚠.
