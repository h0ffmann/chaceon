# 2026-11-04: `W3SIN6` at bit parity

`W3SIN6`, the wind input of `ST6` (Donelan et al. 2006, as Rogers et al. 2012 implement it), and the four helpers of
`W3SRC6MD` it uses (`LFACTOR`, `TAU_WAVE_ATMOS`, `IRANGE`, `TAUWINDS`), translated into `ww3-kernels` with SPIR-V and
PTX entry points. Status `routine` for all five (`rust/port_report/W3SIN6.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings. It
is not called from `W3SRCE`. `W3SPR6`, `W3SDS6`, `W3SWL6` and `W3FLX4` are other ports.

## Reading the compiled code first

The module built alone in `gcc:12-bookworm` (gfortran 12.2) against stub modules, with `-DW3_ST6 -DW3_FLX4` and
`-O3 -ffp-contract=off -fdump-tree-optimized`, gives the GIMPLE of `w3sin6` without building the model. `LFACTOR` is
inlined into it. gfortran calls `sincosf` once, `powif` for `DRTAU**SIGN_NEW`, `powf` for `XFR**(IK10Hz-1.0)`, folds
`GRAV*DWAT` to 9806, writes `-5.E-5*U10 + 1.1E-3` as `1.1E-3 - U10*5.E-5` and keeps `1.0 - (UPROXY*CINV10Hz)` with
its parentheses. The first replay of the first capture was already 0 differ.

## What the captures reach, and what they cannot

`ww3_ts1` at 12 m/s runs the `LFACTOR` iteration (`TAU > TAU_TOT`) in nearly every call, 15 passes at most, and the
negative input (`SINA0 = 0.09`). The grid is extended to 10 Hz (`NK10Hz = 57` against `NK = 36`), so the other branch
needs a spectrum with `NK = 60`. A negative or zero `SINA0` needs a namelist. The viscous stress is zero only above 22
m/s (storm) and is capped at `0.95*TAU_TOT` only in a calm run (`USTAR` floored at 0.001) and in pokes. `ITERMAX` is
out of reach of any run: it takes an entry poke that makes the stress a factor 1E7 too large (`DAIR = 1E7`), where the
iteration needs 81 passes and the warning is written. A negative action, a NaN `USDIR` and a NaN `UABS` are also
pokes, for the `MAX(0.,S)` of `LFACTOR` and the `fmaxnm`/`fminnm` of `W1`, `W2` and `TAU_VIS`.

## Not done

`USTAR = 0`, infinite inputs and `NTH` other than 24 were not run. `ABS(ERR)` exactly at 1.54E-4 and `TAU` exactly
`TAU_TOT` are not reachable. `SDENS10Hz` is dead in the Fortran (it feeds `TAU_NND`, which is only printed): a fault on
it is silent by construction.
