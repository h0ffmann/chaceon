`W3SPR6`, ST6's mean wave parameters and peak frequency, translated into `ww3-kernels` with SPIR-V and PTX entry
points. Status `routine` (`rust/port_report/W3SPR6.md`). Nothing ran on a GPU. No timings. It is not called from `W3SRCE`.

## The poke needed two fixes

The first poke capture scaled the spectrum by `Hs_target/Hs`, but `EMEAN` is linear in `A`, so `Hs` moved as the square root
and no call landed near `HSMIN` ("within 1e-5 of HSMIN" printed 0). The scale is now `(Hs_target/Hs)**2`, with targets
straddling 0.05 by plus or minus 1e-7 and 3e-7: 198 calls land within 1e-5.

A NaN bin at a fixed index did not catch a `MAXVAL` that restarts after a NaN, because the peak lay after that index; with
the NaN at four positions the fault is caught. A fault that nothing catches may be a weak input, not a blind spot.
