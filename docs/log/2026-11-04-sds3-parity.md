## `MAX` is `fmaxnm`

The first kernel returned NaN for a NaN `WNMEAN`. The disassembly of `__w3src3md_MOD_w3sds3` in the reference shows
`fmaxnm s8, s8, s1`, which returns `1.E-10`. The kernel now does the same. The output cannot tell the two apart (NaN
`WNMEAN` gives NaN `S` and `D` through `ALFAMEAN` either way), so the rule rests on the disassembly ⚠.

## What the captures reach

`ww3_ts1` never lets `WNMEAN` fall under the `1.E-10` floor. `st3poke` writes 0, 1e-12, -0.5 and NaN into `WNMEAN` at
the routine's entry; floor and no-floor faults are caught only there. A `DDELTA1` fault is caught on the model captures
but not on the poke, where the `DDELTA2` term dominates by 1e10 (explained in the report).

## Troubles

The capture machine's disk filled at 100% while the first capture ran, which stopped OrbStack; the images survived a
restart. Captures use stride 4, one call in four ⚠.
